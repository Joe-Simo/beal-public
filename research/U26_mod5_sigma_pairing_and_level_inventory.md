## Setting

Let F be the cyclic quartic real field used in the Case-2 computation for exponent 17. In the convention

    F = Q(w),  w^4 - w^3 - 6 w^2 + w + 1 = 0,

one generator of Gal(F/Q) is

    sigma(w) = -w^3/2 + 3 w + 3/2.

The four surviving Hilbert newforms at level norm 272 are

    r = 4.4.4913.1-272.1-r,
    s = 4.4.4913.1-272.1-s,
    v = 4.4.4913.1-272.1-v,
    wform = 4.4.4913.1-272.1-w.

The earlier certified lemma proved, for all four forms,

    f^(sigma^2) = f^tau,

where tau is the relevant coefficient-field automorphism. At the unique ramified residue-degree-one prime lambda above 5, tau acts trivially on the residue field F_5, hence every semisimple residual representation is invariant under sigma^2.

## Exact q=13 sigma action

The polynomial for F splits modulo 13 with roots

    {2, 6, 9, 10}.

Exact modular arithmetic gives

    2 -> 10 -> 6 -> 9 -> 2

under sigma. In the prime ordering used by the q=13 Hecke blocks, this is the 4-cycle `(1 3 2 4)`.

The q=13 blocks in the Michaud-Jacobs computation have the shapes

    r : [e, tau(e), tau(e), e],
    s : [e, tau(e), e, tau(e)],

and

    v : [A, A, e, tau(e)],
    w : [e, tau(e), A, A],

with tau(A)=A. Therefore sigma permutes the forms by

    r^sigma = tau(s),
    v^sigma = w

for this generator orientation; the reverse orientation gives the equivalent conjugate relations. Reducing at lambda, where tau is trivial, yields the residual pairings

    rho_bar_r^sigma  ~= rho_bar_s,
    rho_bar_v^sigma  ~= rho_bar_w.

Together with sigma^2-invariance, this identifies the precise missing step for full Gal(F/Q)-invariance:

    rho_bar_r ~= rho_bar_s

and respectively

    rho_bar_v ~= rho_bar_w.

No such congruence is claimed here.

## Rigorous finite criterion available in the literature

Billerey--Chen--Dembélé--Dieulefait--Freitas, *Some extensions of the modular method and Fermat equations of signature (13,13,n)*, Publ. Mat. 67 (2023), arXiv:1802.04330, Proposition 6.1, gives a rigorous way to certify a residual congruence without a Sturm bound. Their method works in a definite quaternionic Hecke module reduced modulo p: compute the socle for the subalgebra generated by finitely many Hecke operators, prove the relevant simple constituents occur with multiplicity one, and then equality of the corresponding finite Hecke eigensystems implies equality for the full Hecke algebra and hence the semisimple residual Galois representations.

For our problem, the missing executable certificate is therefore concrete: construct the mod-5 Hecke module for the quartic field F at level 272 and test whether the r/s and v/w eigensystems occupy the same multiplicity-one simple constituent. The current environment has no verified Magma runtime, so this certificate has not been executed.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.04330

## Proper lower levels of 2*P_17

A separate exact LMFDB inventory was performed for the same quartic field `4.4.4913.1`. Since the level norm is 272 = 16*17, the proper divisor norms relevant to naive level lowering are 1, 16, and 17. LMFDB currently records:

- norm 1: one weight-2 newform, dimension 2, label `4.4.4913.1-1.1-a`;
