# U82: two additional Frobenius families and a density-537/640 coefficient-support theorem

**Status: written partial theorem with exact finite group certificate and independent Wolfram corroboration. The weighted equation and Beal's conjecture remain unresolved. No independent expert or proof-assistant verification is claimed.**

Research date: 2026-09-16. This continues U81 without altering U80's independent Selmer/class-group bottleneck. U71/U72/U81 are used only at their recorded written/self-reviewed level.

## 1. Scope

Assume a primitive positive solution

    X^10+256Y^10=Z^17.

Retain U71/U72's common integral root

    L=Q(c), c^10=-256, c^5=16i,
    theta=a0+a1*c+...+a9*c^9 in Z[c],
    theta^17=X+cY,

with every coefficient a_j nonzero. Put kappa=|c|=2^(4/5).

U81 called q clean when the residue algebra of T^10+256 has no nontrivial seventeenth roots of unity. Those primes have density 525/640 and satisfy q^(9v_q(Y)) | a9. This checkpoint identifies two disjoint dirty Frobenius families where the local seventeenth root still lies in a one- or two-dimensional subalgebra, so a fixed high coefficient remains strongly divisible.

## 2. The local root expansion

Let q not be 2,5,17 and let e=v_q(Y)>0. Primitivity gives q not dividing X. In the etale Q_q-algebra

    A=Q_q[T]/(T^10+256),

choose the unique exact seventeenth root delta of X that is congruent to theta modulo q in each factor. Such a lift exists because theta itself gives the residual roots and 17 is a q-adic unit. Then theta/delta is congruent to 1 modulo q and

    theta = delta * sum_(n>=0) B_n (cY/X)^n,
    B_n=binomial(1/17,n).                             (2.1)

All B_n are q-adically integral. The only issue is the power-basis support of delta.

## 3. Strong primes: density 529/640

Call q **strong** when either

1. q is U81-clean; or
2. q is congruent to 1 modulo 17 and T^10+256 is irreducible modulo q (Frobenius cycle type 10).

The second family was dirty under U81's definition, but it is just as useful. If q is 1 modulo 17, all seventeenth roots of unity already lie in Q_q. If X acquires a seventeenth root in the degree-ten unramified field A, the Kummer extension generated by that root over Q_q has degree dividing 17 and also dividing 10, hence is trivial. Thus X has a scalar root x in Q_q. Because A is a field and delta/x is a seventeenth root of unity, delta is scalar.

Therefore U81's coefficient calculation applies unchanged:

    v_q(a9)=9e+v_q(658767450) >= 9e.                 (3.1)

U81 contributes 525 of the 640 Frobenius pairs in the compositum of the splitting field with Q(zeta_17). The order-one cyclotomic element together with the four affine 10-cycles adds four more. Hence strong primes have natural density

    529/640.                                          (3.2)

## 4. Semi-strong primes: a two-dimensional local root

Call q **semi-strong** in either of the following disjoint cases:

A. q is congruent to 1 modulo 17 and the factor degrees are (5,5);
B. q has order 2 modulo 17 and T^10+256 is irreducible (cycle type 10).

In both cases the exact local root delta belongs to

    Q_q + Q_q*c^5 = Q_q(i).                           (4.1)

### Case A

The (5,5) affine Frobenius elements have multiplier 1 and nonzero even translation. Since Frobenius fixes c^5=16i, one has i in Q_q, and

    T^10+256=(T^5-16i)(T^5+16i)

with the two irreducible quintic factors. A seventeenth root of X in a degree-five component cannot generate a nontrivial degree-17 Kummer extension of Q_q, so X has a scalar seventeenth root. Because q is 1 modulo 17, the residual/root ambiguity in each component is a scalar seventeenth root of unity. The two componentwise constants are represented by the two idempotents of the displayed factorization, hence by an element d0+d5*c^5.

### Case B

Now 17 does not divide q-1, so X has a unique scalar seventeenth root x in Q_q. The irreducible degree-ten algebra is the unramified degree-ten field. Its unique quadratic subfield is Q_q(i): the cycle-10 translation is odd, so Frobenius negates c^5=16i. Since q has order 2 modulo 17, all seventeenth roots of unity lie in that same unramified quadratic field. Hence delta/x is in Q_q(i), proving (4.1).

Write

    delta=d0+d5*c^5.

Using c^10=-256 in (2.1), the c^9 and c^4 coefficients are

    a9 = d5 * sum_(m>=0) B_(4+10m)(-256)^m (Y/X)^(4+10m)
       + d0 * sum_(m>=0) B_(9+10m)(-256)^m (Y/X)^(9+10m),

    a4 = d0 * sum_(m>=0) B_(4+10m)(-256)^m (Y/X)^(4+10m)
       + d5 * sum_(m>=0) B_(9+10m)(-256)^(m+1) (Y/X)^(9+10m).   (4.2)

Every displayed term has q-adic valuation at least 4e. Therefore

    q^(4e) | a4 and q^(4e) | a9.                    (4.3)

No noncancellation or heuristic root choice is needed for this lower bound.

There are four affine (5,5) elements paired with the unique order-one cyclotomic element, and four affine 10-cycles paired with the unique order-two cyclotomic element. Thus semi-strong primes have density

    8/640 = 1/80.                                    (4.4)

## 5. Combined support theorem

Let Y_s be the strong-prime part of Y and Y_m the semi-strong-prime part. Their supports are disjoint. Equations (3.1) and (4.3) give

    Y_s^9 * Y_m^4 | a9.                              (5.1)

Fourier inversion on the ten complex embeddings gives, as in U81,

    |a9| <= kappa^(-9) (X+kappa Y)^(1/17).

Consequently

    kappa^153 * Y_s^153 * Y_m^68 <= X+kappa Y,

and in particular

    Y_s^153 * Y_m^68 < X+2Y.                         (5.2)

The union of the two controlled prime families has natural density

    (529+8)/640 = 537/640.                            (5.3)

The remaining density is 103/640. This is a support restriction, not an impossibility theorem; an integer can concentrate its support in the remaining set.

## 7. Next mathematical question

The next useful refinement is to inspect the remaining order-one dirty affine classes. In those classes the local root is constant on each Frobenius orbit. When the affine translation is odd, every orbit has equal numbers of even and odd root labels; Fourier inversion then forces the c^5 coefficient of the local root to vanish. This gives a candidate third support tier through the fixed nonzero global coefficient a5. It must be proved and counted before being credited.
