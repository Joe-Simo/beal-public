# U55: coefficient-2 branch — cyclotomic unit collapse and a new difference-of-17th-powers descent

Status: verified partial result. This does not resolve Beal's conjecture.

Date: 2026-09-16.

## 1. Input

For a surviving primitive solution

    X^10+Y^10=2Z^17,

put

    a=X^2,
    b=Y^2.

From U46/U48,

    a^5+b^5=2c^17,
    a+b=2u^17,
    Phi_5(-a,b)=v^17,

with gcd(a,b)=1, 5 does not divide v, and

    a=(m+n)^2,
    b=(m-n)^2,
    m^2+n^2=u^17,
    gcd(m,n)=1,
    m,n opposite parity.

U52 proves the real quadratic factor

    H_-=m^4+(22-8sqrt(5))m^2n^2+n^4

is an untwisted 17th power in Q(sqrt(5)).

## 2. Cyclotomic factorization

Let zeta=zeta_5 and K=Q(zeta). Since

    Phi_5(-a,b)=prod_{j=1}^4 (a+zeta^j b)=v^17,

and common prime-ideal divisors of distinct factors can occur only above 5, while 5 does not divide v, the four factors are pairwise coprime as ideals.

The class number of Q(zeta_5) is 1. Hence each factor is a unit times a 17th power. The unit group is

    mu_10 x <phi>,

where

    phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2.

Because gcd(17,10)=1, the roots-of-unity factor can be absorbed into a 17th power. Thus for some k mod 17,

    a+zeta b = phi^k gamma^17.                       (1)

## 3. The unit exponent is forced globally

Take the relative norm from K to its maximal real subfield K^+=Q(sqrt(5)). Since

    zeta+zeta^(-1)=phi-1,

we get

    N_{K/K+}(a+zeta b)
      = a^2+(phi-1)ab+b^2.                           (2)

With a=(m+n)^2 and b=(m-n)^2, exact symbolic reduction using phi^2=phi+1 gives

    a^2+(phi-1)ab+b^2 = phi^2 H_-.                   (3)

U52 gives H_-=alpha^17 in K^+. On the other hand, taking relative norm in (1) gives

    a^2+(phi-1)ab+b^2 = phi^(2k) N(gamma)^17.

Comparing with (3),

    phi^(2(k-1)) = (alpha/N(gamma))^17.               (4)

The right side is an exact 17th power. Since the left side is a unit, alpha/N(gamma) is a unit of Q(sqrt(5)); its unit group modulo torsion is generated by phi. Therefore 17 divides 2(k-1). As gcd(2,17)=1,

    k == 1 (mod 17).                                  (5)

Thus the cyclotomic unit ambiguity collapses completely.

Now

    1+zeta = (-zeta^3) phi,

and -zeta^3 is a 10th root of unity. Since the 17th-power map is an automorphism of mu_10, this torsion factor can also be absorbed into gamma. Therefore every surviving solution satisfies the exact equation

    X^2+zeta Y^2 = (1+zeta) gamma^17.                 (6)

This is the global explanation for the k=1 class observed in auxiliary-prime experiments; the finite experiments are no longer needed to establish the unit exponent.

## 4. Difference of two 17th powers

From a+b=2u^17,

    u^17=(X^2+Y^2)/2.

Divide (6) by 1+zeta and subtract this rational 17th power:

    gamma^17-u^17
      = (X^2+zeta Y^2)/(1+zeta) - (X^2+Y^2)/2
      = ((1-zeta)/(2(1+zeta)))(X^2-Y^2).

Since X^2-Y^2=4mn, define

    tau=(1-zeta)/(1+zeta).

Then

    gamma^17-u^17 = 2 tau m n.                       (7)

This is a new exact descent equation in Q(zeta_5).

## 5. Coprimality of gamma and u

First,

    gcd(u,mn)=1,

because a rational prime dividing u and one of m,n would divide both m and n via m^2+n^2=u^17.

Also u is odd because m,n have opposite parity.

Finally 5 does not divide u. If 5|u, then m^2+n^2==0 mod5, so the nonzero ratio m/n is +/-2 mod5. U49's exact mod-25 calculation shows precisely in these classes that 5|H(m,n) with v_5(H)=1, impossible because H=v^17.

Suppose a prime ideal P of Q(zeta_5) divides both gamma and the rational integer u. By (7), P divides 2 tau mn. The element tau is supported only at the unique prime above 5 because 1+zeta is a unit and 1-zeta generates the prime above 5. But u is coprime to 2,5,mn. Contradiction. Hence

    (gamma,u)=1                                      (8)

as ideals.

## 6. Cyclotomic factor split at exponent 17

Factor (7):

    gamma^17-u^17
      = (gamma-u) Phi_17(gamma,u)
      = 2 tau mn.                                    (9)

Because of (8), any common prime ideal divisor of gamma-u and Phi_17(gamma,u) divides 17: modulo gamma-u,

    Phi_17(gamma,u) == 17 u^16.

Thus

    gcd((gamma-u), Phi_17(gamma,u))

is supported only above 17.

This turns the right side 2 tau mn into two nearly coprime factors, analogous to the earlier U53 split but now directly in the 17th-power cyclotomic descent.

## 7. Exact local valuations at 2,5,17

### Prime above 2

The prime 2 is inert in Q(zeta_5), with residue field F_16. The 17th-power map on F_16^* has trivial kernel because gcd(17,15)=1. Equation (7) has positive 2-adic valuation, so gamma==u modulo the prime above 2. Since

    Phi_17(gamma,u) == 17u^16

there and u is odd, Phi_17 is a unit. Hence

    v_2(gamma-u) = 1+v_2(mn),                         (10)

where the valuation is normalized by v_P(2)=1.

### Prime pi=(1-zeta) above 5

The denominator 1+zeta is a unit and

    v_pi(tau)=1,
    v_pi(5)=4.

Because 5 does not divide u, equation (7) again forces gamma and u to be pi-adic units with gamma==u mod pi. Since 17 is a pi-adic unit,

    v_pi(Phi_17(gamma,u))=0.

Therefore

    v_pi(gamma-u)=1+4v_5(mn).                         (11)

### Prime above 17

The prime 17 is inert in Q(zeta_5). U54 gives 17|mn while 17 does not divide u. Thus gamma,u are 17-adic units and equation (7) implies gamma^17==u^17 modulo 17. In residue characteristic 17 the 17th-power map is Frobenius and injective, so gamma==u modulo the unique prime above 17.

The local LTE identity for units then gives

    v_17(gamma^17-u^17)=v_17(gamma-u)+1.

Comparing with (7), whose other factors are 17-adic units, yields

    v_17(gamma-u)=v_17(mn)-1,                         (12)
    v_17(Phi_17(gamma,u))=1.

In particular

    v_17(mn)>=2.                                      (13)

This strengthens U54's conclusion 17|mn.

## 8. Current frontier

The coefficient-2 branch now satisfies simultaneously:

    X^2+zeta_5 Y^2=(1+zeta_5)gamma^17,
    gamma^17-u^17=2 tau mn,
    (gamma,u)=1,

with the two factors gamma-u and Phi_17(gamma,u) coprime away from 17 and with exact valuations (10)-(12).

The previously forced primes 67,103,409,883 divide mn, but merely accumulating such divisors is not a proof. The next task is to determine which prime ideals above those rational primes are forced into gamma-u versus Phi_17(gamma,u), and to combine that allocation with the Gaussian equation m+i n=epsilon(r+i s)^17. A successful allocation theorem could force one side of (9) to be a controlled square/17th-power ideal and produce a genuine second descent.
