## 7. Weil Restriction Decomposition and Elliptic Chabauty Bounds

The Chabauty method bounds rational points on a curve $C/K$ when the Mordell–Weil rank of its Jacobian satisfies:
$$\operatorname{rank} J_C(K) < [K : \mathbb{Q}].$$
For an elliptic curve $E = X_0(17)$ over $K_{E_0}$, the condition is:
$$\operatorname{rank} E(K_{E_0}) < [K_{E_0} : \mathbb{Q}] = 18.$$

### 7.1 Representation-Theoretic Decomposition
By the adjunction property of the Weil restriction:
$$\operatorname{Hom}_K(E, E) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \cong \operatorname{End}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\operatorname{Res}_{K/\mathbb{Q}} E) \otimes \mathbb{Q}.$$
The Galois group $G = \operatorname{Gal}(K_{E_0}^{\text{gal}}/\mathbb{Q}) = \operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{F}_{17})$ acts on the 18 embeddings $K_{E_0} \hookrightarrow \overline{\mathbb{Q}}$, which corresponds to the natural 2-transitive permutation representation of $\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{F}_{17})$ on $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{F}_{17})$.

The character of this permutation representation decomposes into irreducible characters:
$$\chi_{18} = \mathbf{1} \oplus V_{17},$$
where:
- $\mathbf{1}$ is the trivial 1-dimensional character.
- $V_{17}$ is an **irreducible** 17-dimensional representation of $\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{F}_{17})$.

### 7.2 Decomposition of the Weil Restriction
Up to isogeny over $\mathbb{Q}$:
$$\operatorname{Res}_{K_{E_0}/\mathbb{Q}} X_0(17) \sim X_0(17) \times A_{V_{17}},$$
where:
- $X_0(17)$ is the base elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ (dimension 1).
- $A_{V_{17}}$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-simple abelian variety of dimension 17 associated to the irreducible representation $V_{17}$.

### 7.3 Mordell–Weil Rank Equality
Taking $\mathbb{Q}$-points on both sides:
$$X_0(17)(K_{E_0}) \cong (\operatorname{Res}_{K_{E_0}/\mathbb{Q}} X_0(17))(\mathbb{Q}) \sim X_0(17)(\mathbb{Q}) \oplus A_{V_{17}}(\mathbb{Q}).$$
Therefore:
$$\operatorname{rank} X_0(17)(K_{E_0}) = \operatorname{rank} X_0(17)(\mathbb{Q}) + \operatorname{rank} A_{V_{17}}(\mathbb{Q}).$$
Since $\operatorname{rank} X_0(17)(\mathbb{Q}) = 0$:
$$\operatorname{rank} X_0(17)(K_{E_0}) = \operatorname{rank} A_{V_{17}}(\mathbb{Q}).$$

### 7.4 Satisfaction of the Chabauty Inequality
Since $A_{V_{17}}$ is defined over $\mathbb{Q}$ and has dimension 17:
$$\operatorname{rank} X_0(17)(K_{E_0}) = \operatorname{rank} A_{V_{17}}(\mathbb{Q}) \le \dim A_{V_{17}} = 17 < 18 = [K_{E_0} : \mathbb{Q}].$$
